# Anatomical soldier head

`human-head.glb` is a modified, cropped MakeHuman adult male mesh with original UV coordinates and a natural photographic-style skin texture. All upstream assets used here are CC0.

- MakeHuman Community, base mesh `hm08`: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
- MakeHuman Community, male shape targets: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/tree/master/makehuman/data/targets/macrodetails (`caucasian-male-young.target`, `universal-male-young-averagemuscle-averageweight.target`).
- Margaret Toigo, `toigo_light_skin_male_freckles` / `young_lightskinned_male_diffuse_Freckles.png`, from the official CC0 male skin pack: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html
- Official asset license explanation: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md — bundled base mesh, targets and textures are CC0; software code has a separate license. The selected community skin is specifically listed as CC0 on the pack page.
- License text saved alongside this file as `LICENSE.CC0.txt`.

Changes: applied adult male shape targets; removed body and helper geometry; normalized the head to roughly 0.29 m facing +Z; subdivided the anatomical surface once; retained the continuous neck down to local y=-0.05 m and bisected/capped its base horizontally; narrowed the lower neck to fit the uniform collar while preserving the front jaw; retained skin UVs; added separate sclera, iris and pupil meshes; embedded the skin image and exported glTF 2.0. Generated by `scripts/build-human-head.py`. No original Call of Duty models or textures are included.
